# Surface inspection

`src/surface.lua` returns a read-only adapter. `inspect(bounds, owner, access)`
accepts inclusive integer bounds using `min_x`, `max_x`, `min_y`, `max_y`,
`min_z`, and `max_z`. Its access object provides `get_node(pos)`,
`has_metadata(pos)`, `is_protected(pos, owner)`, and
`get_natural_light(pos, time_of_day)`; missing results are
unknown. `engine_access(core)` provides these functions for Luanti. The caller
must emerge the bounded patch and apply survey budgets before invoking it. The
inspector additionally rejects volumes over two million nodes. It reads the
whole vertical window, one column at a time, and keeps only summaries afterward.
It performs no map writes. Its snapshot getter makes no engine calls.

The adapter deliberately recognizes only specific VoxeLibre node names. Other
nodes, including metadata-bearing nodes and tree trunks, are obstacles. It
cannot distinguish a natural block placed by a player from the same block
generated by the map. Water or a structure above the natural surface rejects
the column. Canopy may be inspected through, but remains in the band. Unknown
nodes anywhere in the inspection window and clipped bands reject the column.
The top node must be air with natural light 15 at time 0.5; missing, lower,
or failed light reads yield unknown. This bounded noon-light check is a
practical outdoor signal, not proof of an unobstructed sky: transparent roofs
and stale lighting can cause misclassification. Artificial light must not be
substituted. The light read is at the same top-window node, with no additional
emerge or unbounded scan.

`player_feet(player)` converts the player reference position using the player's
collision box. `endpoints(snapshot, first_feet, second_feet)` checks both
requested positions and the separately inspected lattice snap and returns
`{start=Endpoint, goal=Endpoint}` or `nil, error`. The complete endpoint caps
still require planner feasibility checks.

Read-only integration check: Luanti 5.18.0, VoxeLibre 0.92.3, seed
3718742572506294903, a disposable world, column (0,0), Y -16 through 32.
After `emerge_area`, the engine-backed inspector reported `water`, consistent
with the generated ocean column. The probe called no map-writing APIs; the
engine generated the disposable world. This is a narrow engine/API smoke test,
not a survey of all biomes or player-placed materials.

After adding the natural-light check, a second disposable flat-mapgen world
(seed 9150473146606508302) was inspected with the engine-backed adapter. For
column (9,0), window Y=3..17, natural light at (9,17,0) at noon was 15 and the
result was `ok`, ground Y=12, material `mcl_core:dirt_with_grass`. The probe
generated disposable terrain and made only read calls during inspection.
